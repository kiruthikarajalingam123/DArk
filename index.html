Rulebook

Objective

Survive until the timer ends and maximize your score by defeating enemies and clearing rooms.

Scoring

Player scores +100 points per enemy eliminated
Bonus money depends on enemy type.

Enemy Types

Guard
Patrols between two points. Switches to chase mode if it sees player or when the player enters its room.
Shoots at regular intervals when chasing.Your currency increases by $15 for eliminating a guard.

Sentry
Does not move. Rotates in place scanning for the player.
Fires when the player is detected in vision or when the player enters its room. Your currency increases by $10 for eliminating a guard.

Hunter
Actively chases the player once detected.
Has longer detection range and keeps chasing for a duration even if it loses sight.
More dangerous and worth more points. Your currency increases by $25 for eliminating a guard.

Rooms

The map is divided into rooms connected by doors. Clearing a room happens when all enemies inside are defeated.

You can also add 15 seconds to your game by spending $30 of game currency.

Winning Condition

There is no “end level”. Your goal is to survive the full time limit and achieve the highest possible score.
